## Delivery, Assembly and Installation

### Delivery

Express delivery is not available. Free delivery is not available—delivery is charged. The minimum order amount for delivery is AED 100.

**Delivery times:**
- Dubai and Sharjah: 2–3 business days.
- Other emirates: 2–5 business days.

Delivery charges depend on the order amount and the emirate. There are two pricing groups: Dubai and all other emirates. **IMPORTANT:** Sharjah is included in the "all other emirates" pricing group, although delivery to Sharjah takes 2–3 days.

#### Delivery charges — Dubai

| Order amount (AED) | Delivery charge (AED) |
| --- | --- |
| 100–999 | 50 |
| 1,000–4,000 | 100 |
| 4,001–10,000 | 200 |
| 10,001–30,000 | 600 |
| 30,001–50,000 | 900 |
| 50,001–100,000 | 1,500 |
| Above 100,000 | Please contact the manager |

#### Delivery charges — all other emirates

Includes Sharjah. Minimum charge: AED 200.

| Order amount (AED) | Delivery charge (AED) |
| --- | --- |
| 100–999 | 200 |
| 1,000–4,000 | 250 |
| 4,001–10,000 | 400 |
| 10,001–30,000 | 800 |
| 30,001–50,000 | 1,200 |
| 50,001–100,000 | 2,000 |

#### Full truck delivery

For heavy or large orders; available only in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. The total order weight is required.

| Weight | Dubai (AED) | Abu Dhabi (AED) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up to 1 tonne | 130 | 300 |
| 1–3 tonnes | 300 | 400 |
| Above 3 tonnes | Please contact the manager | Please contact the manager |

Outside the UAE (Saudi Arabia, Oman and Qatar): delivery and installation are arranged through a manager.

### Assembly and installation

- **Display items**: AED 30 per item; this service can be selected optionally during checkout.
- **Projects and made-to-order furniture**: installation is included in the project cost.
