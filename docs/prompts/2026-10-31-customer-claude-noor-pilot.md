Identify the exact component that can enforce one owner at the FINAL send boundary. An inbound-only filter or a forwarded copy is insufficient if a second assistant can still send a queued answer.

Define the agreed new-lead boundary, atomic assignment, duplicate handling and ownership transfers. Retain assignments across retries, restarts and date changes. 3. Establish a jointly supported assignment/egress contract with the Noor team. 5. Keep human takeover and emergency stop explicit and visible to both systems. If assignment or Noor fails, hold/alert according to the agreed policy; do not automatically let both assistants reply or replay captured history.
